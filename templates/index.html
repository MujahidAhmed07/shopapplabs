Frequently Asked Questions

What is a username checker?

A username checker is a free online tool that instantly checks whether a specific username or handle is available across multiple social media platforms and websites at the same time.

How do I check if a username is available on Instagram?

Type your desired username in the search box above and press Search. The tool instantly shows whether your handle is available or taken on Instagram along with 100+ other platforms.

Which platforms does this checker support?

Over 100 platforms: Instagram, TikTok, X (Twitter), YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, GitHub, Twitch, Reddit, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Spotify, SoundCloud, Steam, and domain extensions (.com, .net, .org, .io, .dev).

Is this username checker free?

Yes — completely free. No sign-up, no account, no limits required.

How accurate are the results?

The tool queries each platform’s public API or profile URL in real-time. Results are highly accurate but may show ‘Unknown’ for platforms with rate limits or restricted APIs.

Can I check domain name availability?

Yes. Switch to the Domains tab to check .com, .net, .org, .io, .co, and .dev availability simultaneously based on your searched handle.
